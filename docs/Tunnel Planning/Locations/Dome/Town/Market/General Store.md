This is the main [[Shop]] which is unlocked right after your [[First Expedition]]. You can buy and sell a few things here. Not everything is initially available. It is run by the [[Shopkeeper]].

Choices:
* You can sell your [[Items/Gathered/Flowers/Lemen|Lemens]] for a meager number of [[Coin|Coins]]
* This is where you buy your initial [[Items/Major Items/Equipment|Equipment]], although now that the requirement for needing it is moved later, I might be able to put this in a shop in [[Explorer's Alley]] instead
* You can buy some Basic Flower Seeds here before you've unlocked [[Aunt Petunia's Garden Shop]]

There are also some special choices that unlock in certain scenarios.
* You can sell your [[Clackadoo Feather]] that [[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/George|George]] gave you during your [[Last Training Expedition]] for a ridiculous number of [[Coin|Coins]], though this comes with consequences later (I plan some involving [[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/Troy|Troy]])

There are some items you can only buy a limited number of times:
* At the start, you can buy the shopkeeper's hat for a ridiculous amount of [[Coin|Coins]] (because it's so dapper), but it turns out to be junk (he even gives it to you for free) and you throw it out, after which the option is gone (maybe he has a hat in the image at first, and it disappears after this option)
* You can buy a couple [[Petunal Blossoms]] here if you need some in a pinch; they are expensive though, and the shopkeeper only has a limited supply
