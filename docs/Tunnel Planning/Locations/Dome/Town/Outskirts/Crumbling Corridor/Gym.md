When you enter the gym, you are met with the bark of a loud, fluffy, large certified good boy. This pupper needs some love!

You can pet the dog when you get to the gym. The dog's name is [[Varik]], and I don't have rewards planned for petting him, but there is the intrinsic reward of knowing you pet a dog (even if it was a fictional one).

There are a variety of training programs. A program consists of a certain number of visits, which must be completed at regular intervals (for example, you might need to train ever 5-10 [[Day|Days]] for a certain number of days). If you miss a training, then you must pay the costs again to restart. They usually have large benefits to your [[Physical]] skill. They're very [[Mechanics/Major/Vigor/Vigor|Vigor]] intensive.
