The Foyer is the place you return to after each [[Expeditions|Expedition]], and connects to most other locations in the [[Locations/Dome/Orphanage/Orphanage]], and also the locations outside.

Choices:
- Go to [[Admin]] area (requires high [[Sneakiness]])
- Go to [[Commons]] area (unlocked from start)
- Go to [[Rooms]] (unlocked from start)
- Go [[Mechanics/Progression/Volunteering|Volunteering]] (unlocked from start)
- Go into [[Locations/Dome/Town/Town|Town]] (unlocked from start)
- Go on an [[Expeditions|Expedition]] (unlocked once you finish [[Training]] with [[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/George|George]], until then you have to go to [[Locations/Dome/Orphanage/Rooms/George|George's Room]] to go on an [[Expeditions|Expedition]]

This does not connect all locations. For example, there is no connection to the [[Basement]], which are reached from the [[Rooms]].
