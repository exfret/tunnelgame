The [[Locations/Tunnel/Tunnel|Tunnel]], from which [[Storyline/Overview/Tunnel|Tunnel]] gets its name, is the only exit to the [[World/Places/Dome|Dome]] in which humanity is confined. People have long forgotten what "old" life was like, especially since no one has found a way through the [[Locations/Tunnel/Tunnel|Tunnel]] in centuries. Many think it to be infinite even.
