Mostly focused around some core things that are built on a lot as you go, such as gatherables ([[Botany]] is relevant here), or [[Mechanics/Major/Research/Level 1 Research Lines/Artifacts/Artifacts|Artifacts]]. The first volume of all of these is available at the [[Study]].

The following are immediately researchable:
- [[Writing I]]
- [[Creatures I]]
- [[Reading I]]
- [[Exploration I]]

The following can be gotten after [[Reading I]]:
- [[Artifacts I]]
- [[Botany I]]
- [[Cartography I]]

The following require [[Reading I]] and for additional books to arrive per the [[Unlocks|Unlock]] for it tied to [[Experience]]:
- [[Construction I]]
- [[Observation I]]

The following requires just for the additional books [[Unlocks|Unlock]]:
- [[Survival I]]
- [[Fitness I]]

Finally, [[Combat I]] is unlocked simply by reaching the right age.

I'm thinking you can get a library pass to [[Lengthwidth Library]] from [[Mabel]] after 2 researches requiring [[Reading I]] are completed.
