You can research different upgrades by reading a book. At first, this involves paying [[Mabel]] some [[Items/Gathered/Flowers/Rosalia|Rosalia]] to rent a book from the [[Study]]. Later, you can visit the [[Lengthwidth Library]] in [[Town Square]] for research, and the most advanced research must be done in the [[City]].

Since there are so many research lines, I've categorized them into levels.
1. Level 1 research lines can be found at the [[Study]], they have further volumes at [[Lengthwidth Library]]
2. Level 2 research lines are too advanced to be found at the [[Study]], instead their first volumes are found at [[Lengthwidth Library]]
3. Level 3 research lines are found only after a lot of reserach at [[Lengthwidth Library]] or found in special areas like in [[Society Circle]] or at a university maybe if I code one
4. Level 4 research lines need to be started in the [[City]] or as very special rewards in the [[Locations/Dome/Town/Town|Town]] or [[World/Places/Tunnel|Tunnel]], though I haven't really planned that far ahead yet
Thus, volume III of a level 2 research is as advanced as volume I of a level 4 research (so both would be probably found in the City).
