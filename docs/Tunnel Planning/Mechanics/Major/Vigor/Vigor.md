You have a certain amount of vigor, which represents your overall enthusiasm and life outlook. You can increase it via [[Long Rest|Long Rests]] at the [[Locations/Dome/Orphanage/Orphanage]], which decreases [[Experience]] by a certain amount. It is used like a currency for a few things, such as [[World/Places/Tunnel|Tunnel]] [[Expeditions]], and especially [[Jobs|Jobs]]. There is a [[Mechanics/Major/Vigor/Max Vigor|Max Vigor]], which can be upgraded later, especially with [[Mechanics/Major/Vigor/Vigor Points|Vigor Points]].
