There's a vigor point skill tree. You can use vigor points for upgrading your [[Mechanics/Major/Vigor/Max Vigor|Max Vigor]], decreasing [[Experience]] lost during [[Long Rest|Long Rests]], and other similar things. You gain vigor points through "focusing" at different [[Place|Places]] outside the [[Locations/Tunnel/Tunnel|Tunnel]], which usually just requires unlocking the [[Place]], but some require something related to that [[Place]] to focus in. For example, to focus in the [[Locations/Dome/Orphanage/Orphanage|Orphanage's]] [[Locations/Dome/Orphanage/Commons/Gardens|Gardens]] may require harvesting a certain number of plants first. This rewards exploration of different spots in the game.
