A good source of [[Currencies|Currency]], though most of these cost a lot of [[Mechanics/Major/Vigor/Vigor|Vigor]]. You can get committed jobs as well. There is no requirement to actually work at your job, but you can only hold so many at a time, and if you leave, you don't get to keep a lot of your progress down the job line. Thus, it is beneficial to stick with a career or two and only switch when it makes sense.
