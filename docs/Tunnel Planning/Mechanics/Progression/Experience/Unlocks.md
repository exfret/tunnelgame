This lists a basic draft of what might unlock at various experience levels.

1. (5 Exp) The ability to return from an expedition early
2. (10 Exp) The ability to participate in [[Mechanics/Progression/Volunteering|Volunteering]] trips
3. (15 Exp) Access to the orphanage's [[Locations/Dome/Orphanage/Commons/Gardens|Gardens]]
4. (20 Exp) Some sort of increased control in where you end up during an [[Encounters|Encounter]]
5. (25 Exp) Access to [[Explorer's Alley]], which initially just has a trainer (unwritten at the moment) who can teach you in a couple skills
6. (30 Exp) More interaction options with other characters?
7. (35 Exp) Access to a [[Shovel]], with the first level of it representing that you own one (you can't upgrade without it)
8. (40 Exp) Access to [[Market Square]] and in it [[Aunt Petunia's Garden Shop]] and the market's [[Booths]]
9. (45 Exp) Access to [[Outskirts]] and the first area in it, [[Crumbling Corridor]]
10. (50 Exp) The ability to progress beyond [[Expeditions|Expedition]] 8 to visit the [[Woods]] (you can't go on more training expeditions until you've gotten this experience), with the explanation from [[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/George|George]] that it's because the next area is more dangerous
11. (60 Exp) Access to a [[Pickaxe]]
12. (70 Exp) Having high [[Mechanics/Major/Vigor/Vigor|Vigor]] can cause you to notice more things on [[Expeditions]]
13. (80 Exp) Access to the [[Blacksmith]] in [[Explorer's Alley]] who can upgrade things like your [[Shovel]] and [[Pickaxe]]
14. (90 Exp) Advanced Farmhand job
15. (100 Exp) Ability to get [[Mechanics/Major/Vigor/Vigor Points|Vigor Points]]
16. (110 Exp) The [[Study]] gets more books
17. (120 Exp) Additional increased control over which [[Encounters|Encounter]] you get, maybe something like being able to choose the first [[Encounters|Encounter]] from a [[Bucket]]
18. (130 Exp) The [[General Store]] gets more items
19. (140 Exp) Another area in town/the orphanage?
20. (150 Exp) Some places/people that wanted this amount of experience to interact with them are now unlocked
21. (160 Exp) Some gathering skills, such as [[Lemen Gather Skill]]
22. (170 Exp) Access to the next area in the [[Outskirts]], the [[Farmer's Flatlands]]
23. (180 Exp) Unlocks previously hidden choices in some [[Encounters]]
24. (190 Exp) ??
25. (200 Exp) Ability to attend [[Events]], and visit the [[Locations/Dome/Town/Town|Town's]] [[Event Board]]
26. (240 Exp) Access to the next area in the [[Outskirts]], the [[Forbidding Field]]
27. (280 Exp) Some gifts and important pieces of progression (like a [[Square Hat]]) if you don't already have them, also new [[Clothes]] (maybe the gifts are more if you already had the progression items)
28. (300 Exp) Needed for a [[Blue Pass]]
