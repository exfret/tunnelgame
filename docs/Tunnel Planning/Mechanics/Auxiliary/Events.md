Events happen at predetermined, fixed times in the story, usually every [[Year]]. They are only available for a [[Week]] or two, so you have to plan your [[Expeditions]] around them if you want to go. Each event can only be visited once, even if it's still around. I'm thinking you can have a tracker/calendar where you can see what events are happening soon so you don't miss them. A goal with this mechanic is to increase the sense of the passage of [[Time]].
