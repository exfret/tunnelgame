Some [[Encounters]] have "vertical depth", where each visit something new happens in the same location. Others have "horizontal depth", where each visit you can explore a new part of the [[Encounters|Encounter]]. Either way, the vast majority of encounters should have a lot of some type of depth.

Vertical depth itself can be split up into different types:
- Visits-based: Each time you visit, a different thing happens, eventually looping back to the first thing, repeating things in a [[Bucket]]-based manner, or just sticking with the final variation
- Reaction-based: Based off other events, or how you show up to the [[Encounters|Encounter]] (like what items you have in your pocket), the [[Encounters|Encounter]] plays out differently
- Upgrade-based: Similar to the [[Treehouse]] [[Encounters|Encounter]], as you come across the [[Encounters|Encounter]] multiple times, you can "upgrade" some aspects of it, leading to new and improved interactions

Horizontal depth can also be split into types:
- Linear, where you progress through different parts of the [[Encounters|Encounter]], such as how you go from the [[Grave]] to the [[Field]] in that one
- Branching, where initially you have many choices of where to go, each one having its own things to do

As [[Encounters]] themselves can have subareas, subsubareas, etc., they may have a "macro" level complexity that is one way, like an area that progresses in a linearly horizontal manner, but then each subarea along the progression is maybe visits-based, or some other combination.
