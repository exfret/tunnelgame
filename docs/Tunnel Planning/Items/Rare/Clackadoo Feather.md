ID: inv_clackadoo

 Clackadoo feathers come from the clackadoo bird, which is native to all [[World/Places/Layers|Layers]] past the first two. The clackadoo bird has an innate sense of impending danger, which makes it incredibly hard to catch. It also infuses the feather with the distinctive ability to blow away whenever mortal danger is nearby, making it very useful.

You get one from [[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/George|George]] as a gift at the end of your [[Training]] ([[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/George|George]] himself got it from his father [[Characters/Tso|Tso]]).
