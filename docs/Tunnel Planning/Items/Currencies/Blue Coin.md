Blue coins are a type of currency used more by [[World/Places/Tunnel|Tunnel]] explorers, as that is where they are found. This is in contrast to [[Coin|Coins]], which are mined in the [[World/Places/Dome|Dome's]] [[Quarry]]. Some [[World/Places/Tunnel|Tunnel]] explorers exclusively accept blue coins, considering them superior due to coming from the [[World/Places/Tunnel|Tunnel]].
