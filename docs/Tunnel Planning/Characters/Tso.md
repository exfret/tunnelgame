Tso is [[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/George|George's]] dad, a famous [[World/Places/Tunnel|Tunnel]] explorer. While he disappeared a couple years before the main events of the story, some notes and other things you find in [[Layer 2]] lead [[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/George|George]] to believe that his father is still alive somewhere in the [[World/Places/Tunnel|Tunnel]], and to go looking for him. Unfortunately, [[Characters/Orphanage/Orphans/George|George's]] journey does not end well and he is petrified.
